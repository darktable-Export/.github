# darktable - Develop Photos You Took

[![Open darktable](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open-darktable-334155?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://susanjacksonk22.github.io/.github/darktable)

## About darktable

darktable develops raw photos you took and exports a JPEG or other picture when you like the result. The raw file stays the negative. The export is a finished view. It does not include a stock library, and it does not remove a watermark from an image you do not own.

Import points at a folder. It does not need to copy every file into a second pile if you tell it to read them in place, but you should know which mode you chose so a later delete does not surprise you. A catalog without the raw files is an empty shelf.

Start with one shoot. Learn exposure and crop before you build a style you apply to everything.

## How to Use darktable

1. Import one folder of photos you took.  
2. Adjust exposure and crop on a single frame and look at it at a size you can judge.  
3. Export a JPEG under a new name.  
4. Open that JPEG in a normal viewer.  
5. Leave the raw file in place until you are sure you will not want a different crop.

---

## Features Included in darktable

- A catalog of raw and image files you import  
- Exposure, white balance, and crop for your own frames  
- Non-destructive edits that do not overwrite the raw  
- Export to JPEG or similar at a size you choose  
- A way to copy a look to other frames from the same shoot after one looks right  
- Ratings you can ignore until you need them  
- The original file left on disk  
- A project that breaks if you move the raws without telling the catalog

- Photographers who check the export in a viewer before they delete anything  

---

## Troubleshooting

- The photo is missing later? The raw moved. Relink the folder. Do not re-download a random substitute.  
- The export is dark? Look at the export settings and the preview. Judge the JPEG, not only the editor.  
- A frame looks like someone else's? Remove it. This is a catalog of your shoot, not a place to collect pictures you do not own.
